Status before the search: `NOT_RUN`. This search is limited to the packaged `Town03`, `Town02_Opt`, and `Town05_Opt` OpenDRIVE files and the existing 1 m offline CARLA GlobalRoutePlanner. It does not connect to or start a CARLA server, read TEST routes or payloads, capture an episode, use SSH/GPU, install software, or change any acceptance threshold. The exact output is a new `report.json` in this directory; existing catalogs and episodes are immutable.

The site roles are provisional. Town03 is the driving research DEV map, with the existing `right_s0000_p00` DEV episode used as a same-junction rejection reference. Town02_Opt is a proposed TRAIN map in the latest integrated map plan, but its driving split is not mechanically locked. Town05_Opt has historical TRAIN exposure and a newer DEV proposal, so no route there can be admitted to an independent DEV without resolving that conflict. Town04/Town15 and reserved/held-out Town06, Town07 and Town10HD_Opt are excluded; Town13 has an unresolved live rendering failure and is outside this search.

For every 60–120° right-heading driving connector, search all endpoint combinations from `entry.previous(u)` and `exit.next(d)` for the Cartesian grid `u={50,60,70,80,90,100} m`, `d={80,100,120,140,160} m`. Serialize at 1 m resolution. Require length ≥146 m (`120 m` fixed window + `16 m` at 9 km/h for the unchanged 6.4 s future + `10 m` guard), traversal of the chosen connector, exactly one contiguous RIGHT block and no other maneuver, and the turn entirely within the unchanged 30–120 m window. Apply the original `speed_30kph` **geometry profile** without alteration; its name is not approval to drive at 30 km/h. Record source hashes, stage counts, every physical-profile PASS route, full-route signal-reference roads, other junction roads in the protected 30–146 m span, and Town03 same-junction overlap.

Any static candidate remains provisional until its exact live XODR is matched, full signal approach and nominal/actual Prius body plus 10 cm inset sweep are checked, and a separately owned original expert episode passes continuous `>0.5 m/s` movement for ≥30 s in the fixed window, all 64 future points/6.4 s, the original nine motion bounds, collision/lane checks, and a ≤2.5 m goal. This offline search grants neither capture nor dataset admission, training, or learned closed-loop credit.
